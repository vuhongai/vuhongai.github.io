### AAV Capsid Language Models

<div class="focus-card" style="margin-bottom:1.5rem;">
<div class="focus-icon">🤖</div>
<h3>AI-Driven Capsid Design</h3>
<p>
We are developing encoder and decoder transformer models trained on the full diversity of natural and engineered AAV capsid sequences. These models learn the sequence–function landscape of AAV, enabling <strong>in silico prediction</strong> of fitness, tropism, manufacturing yield, and immunogenicity before any experiment is run.
</p>
<p style="margin-top:0.6rem !important;">
<strong>Status:</strong> Active — models in training and wet-lab validation ongoing<br>
<strong>Partners:</strong> Genethon, University Paris-Saclay
</p>
</div>

### FIBER Matching — Muscle-Targeted AAV Variants

<div class="focus-card" style="margin-bottom:1.5rem;">
<div class="focus-icon">🧬</div>
<h3>FIBER: Fiber-Inspired Binding Engineering for Retargeting</h3>
<p>
Rational engineering of the AAV capsid fiber-like surface loops to redirect tropism toward skeletal muscle and cardiac tissue. We use structure-guided mutagenesis informed by cryo-EM data and integrin-binding motifs (RGD, NGR) to produce capsids with superior muscle selectivity across species — mouse, non-human primate, and human primary cells.
</p>
<p style="margin-top:0.6rem !important;">
<strong>Key result:</strong> Lead variant published in <em>Nature Communications</em> (2024) — first AAV targeting integrin αVβ6 with improved myotropism across species<br>
<strong>Status:</strong> Active — next-generation variants in preclinical evaluation<br>
<strong>IP:</strong> PCT/EP2024/082609 · PCT/EP2023/065499
</p>
</div>

### Immune Evasion — IgG Degrader Proteins

<div class="focus-card" style="margin-bottom:1.5rem;">
<div class="focus-icon">🛡️</div>
<h3>Engineered IgG-Degrading Enzymes for AAV Re-Administration</h3>
<p>
Pre-existing neutralizing antibodies (NAbs) against AAV remain the main barrier to treating seropositive patients. We are engineering <strong>artificial IgG-degrading proteins</strong> (inspired by IdeS/IdeZ) with optimized substrate specificity and safety profiles to transiently deplete anti-AAV NAbs and enable repeated dosing.
</p>
<p style="margin-top:0.6rem !important;">
<strong>Status:</strong> Active — lead candidates in cell-based neutralization assays<br>
<strong>IP:</strong> EP24306388 · EP25306380.4
</p>
</div>

### Dystrophin Optimization — Dual-AAV Midi-Dystrophins

<div class="focus-card" style="margin-bottom:1.5rem;">
<div class="focus-icon">💊</div>
<h3>Computational Design of Optimized Midi-Dystrophins</h3>
<p>
Full-length dystrophin exceeds the AAV packaging capacity (~4.7 kb). We apply <strong>structural prediction (AlphaFold2) and sequence modeling</strong> to design compact midi-dystrophins that retain maximum functional domains while fitting within a dual-AAV intein-mediated split strategy.
</p>
<p style="margin-top:0.6rem !important;">
<strong>Key result:</strong> Published in <em>International Journal of Molecular Sciences</em> (2024)<br>
<strong>Status:</strong> Preclinical validation ongoing in <em>mdx</em> mice
</p>
</div>

### miRNA Biomarkers in Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy

<div class="focus-card" style="margin-bottom:1.5rem;">
<div class="focus-icon">📊</div>
<h3>circulating miRNA Signatures as DMD Biomarkers</h3>
<p>
We characterized circulating and muscle-expressed microRNA profiles in DMD patients and mouse models (mdx, mdx-4cv). The Dlk1-Dio3 miRNA cluster emerged as a major regulator of mitochondrial function in dystrophic muscle — a potential therapeutic target and disease-progression biomarker.
</p>
<p style="margin-top:0.6rem !important;">
<strong>Key publications:</strong> <em>Life Science Alliance</em> (2023) · <em>J. Cachexia, Sarcopenia & Muscle</em> (2021, 2022)<br>
<strong>Status:</strong> Completed — follow-up translational studies in progress
</p>
</div>
