Building on the first-generation LICA1 vector, we combined structural modeling with protein-AI models to design a second generation of muscle-targeted AAV capsids. Two lead vectors, LICA3 and LICA4, deliver highly efficient gene transfer to both skeletal and cardiac muscle at low systemic doses — exceeding one vector genome per nucleus at doses roughly twenty times below those used in current clinical trials — while preserving compatibility with standard manufacturing. This work demonstrates that AI-guided design can optimize multiple vector properties at once, accelerating the development of clinically relevant gene-delivery vectors.
